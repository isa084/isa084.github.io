Background

My experience spans processor and SoC architecture, pre-silicon verification, CPU/cache/memory analysis, FPGA prototyping, embedded Linux, hardware/software integration, and performance investigation. I have worked across automotive and edge systems, networking and infrastructure compute, cryptographic computing, and open-source processor engineering.

I am particularly interested in problems that require controlled experiments and deep debugging across subsystem boundaries: identifying whether behaviour comes from the workload, compiler, processor, memory system, operating system, or the measurement method itself.

Selected technical work

  • RISC-V ISA-extension and CVA6 microarchitecture work, including RTL integration, simulation, synthesis, FPGA evaluation, and Linux-capable platforms.
  • CPU, cache, memory, accelerator, and interconnect verification and performance analysis across complex SoCs.
  • Reusable automation for regressions, benchmark experiments, simulation analysis, and AI-assisted engineering workflows.
